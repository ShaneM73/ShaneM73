# 👋 Hi, I'm Shane

A map of my own projects, grouped by theme.

## 🌍 Geospatial Science

### &emsp;&emsp;Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
- [MO-wetland2026](https://github.com/ShaneM73/MO-wetland2026) — Missouri wetland mapping pilot: terrain wetness, NISAR radar and drone LiDAR for Strother Creek and Barton Fen

### &emsp;&emsp;Historical Geography

- [Chinese_spatiotemporal_geography](https://github.com/ShaneM73/Chinese_spatiotemporal_geography) — 3D space–time cube of the borders, events and place names of China and its neighbours, in 4 languages

### &emsp;&emsp;Campus Atlases
- [msu-campus-atlas](https://github.com/ShaneM73/msu-campus-atlas) — 3D infographic of the Missouri State campus, built from the campus SketchUp model
- [msu-campus-atlas-2026](https://github.com/ShaneM73/msu-campus-atlas-2026) — 3D atlas of the Missouri State campus, built from current OpenStreetMap data
- [oakland-campus-atlas](https://github.com/ShaneM73/oakland-campus-atlas) — 3D atlas of Oakland, Pittsburgh, comparing the Pitt and Carnegie Mellon campuses

### &emsp;&emsp;3D Modeling
- [Blunt_hall_3D](https://github.com/ShaneM73/Blunt_hall_3D) — web viewer for handheld LiDAR scans of Blunt Hall: point clouds, SLAM floor plans and a 3D mesh
- [computer-graphics-explorables](https://github.com/ShaneM73/computer-graphics-explorables) — step-through teaching modules on ray tracing and Gaussian splatting

### &emsp;&emsp;Economic Landscape
- [job-market-map](https://github.com/ShaneM73/job-market-map) — 3D map of the 2026 US labor market and the top hubs for geospatial jobs, with a research report
- [market-pulse-dashboard](https://github.com/ShaneM73/market-pulse-dashboard) — S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq-100 heat maps plus a 3D map of where index companies are headquartered

### &emsp;&emsp;Social GIS
- [world-news-globe](https://github.com/ShaneM73/world-news-globe) — 3D globe of the day's biggest news stories, updated automatically every day from GDELT
- [Springfield-flow](https://github.com/ShaneM73/Springfield-flow) — 3D space–time cube of commuting flows in the Springfield, MO metro, 2002–2024, from LEHD LODES: in-state and out-of-state commuters, job centres and commuting communities ([launch](https://shanem73.github.io/Springfield-flow/))

### &emsp;&emsp;GEO 360 Aerial Photo Interpretation
- [color-theory](https://github.com/ShaneM73/color-theory) — Color Theory Lab: additive and subtractive color, color models and perception in eight interactive steps
